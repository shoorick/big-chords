Comma-prefixed chord lines

With data-detect-chords="comma", a line starting with a comma is treated as a chord line; the comma itself is replaced by a space. Any other line is rendered as plain text. With data-internal-markup="mediawiki", lyric lines also support ''italic'' and '''bold''' (see ''Припев:'' below).

Songs formatted this way can be found on bard-wiki.net — the song below is taken from there.
